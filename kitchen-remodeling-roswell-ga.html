    Frequently Asked Questions

    How long does a kitchen remodel take in Roswell?

    Most kitchen remodeling projects in Roswell take between 4 and 8 weeks, depending on the scope, structural changes, and material lead times. Homes with layout reconfiguration or custom finishes may require up to 12 weeks.

    Are permits required for kitchen remodeling in Roswell?

    Yes. The City of Roswell requires permits for electrical, plumbing, and structural modifications. Bento’s Group manages the full permitting process, inspections, and any HOA coordination to ensure compliance from start to finish.

    Can I remain in my home during the renovation?

    In most cases, yes. We plan renovations in phases, use dust containment systems, and protect adjacent living areas to keep your home functional throughout the project.

    Is Bento’s Group licensed and insured in Georgia?

    Absolutely. We are fully licensed, bonded, and insured in the state of Georgia, with 5.0 Google rating and a long track record of completed projects across North Fulton County.
