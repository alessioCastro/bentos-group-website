Philosophy

Beyond Aesthetics: The Science of Kitchen Layouts.

At Bento's Group, we believe the kitchen is a precision-engineered workspace. Our designs are rooted in the "Working Triangle" principle—minimizing the distance between the sink, refrigerator, and stovetop to reduce unnecessary movement.

We analyze Kitchen Ergonomics to ensure counter heights, cabinet depths, and clearance zones (at least 42" for single-cook kitchens) are optimized for your physical comfort. Every structural modification we execute as a Licensed General Contractor is designed to enhance both daily utility and long-term resale value.

Material Engineering

Technical specifications of the components that build a Bento's Group kitchen.

01

Advanced Cabinetry

We specify furniture-grade plywood carcasses (no particle board) with Dovetail Joinery. Our finish options include moisture-resistant conversion varnishes and catalyzed lacquers that withstand the rigors of high-heat cooking environments.

  • Soft-close BLUM Hinges
  • Sustainable Wood Sourcing
02

Luxury Surfaces

From Calacatta Gold Quartz to Leathered Granite, our stone selection focuses on MOHS hardness and non-porosity. We utilize laser-templating for zero-clearance seams and mitered "waterfall" edges that flow into the floor.

  • Antimicrobial Sealing
  • High Heat Tolerance
03

Lighting Layers

We implement Three-Layer Lighting: Task (under-cabinet), Ambient (recessed cans), and Accent (toe-kick and pendant). All systems are integrated with smart dimmers for total atmosphere control.

  • CRI 90+ LED Systems
  • App-Controlled Dimmers
